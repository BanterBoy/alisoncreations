Terms & Conditions

The essentials for purchasing Alison Resin Creations handmade resin art.

Orders

All orders are processed via Shopify. Once checkout is completed and payment is authorised you will receive an email confirmation. Custom orders are confirmed separately via email before payment is taken.

Pricing

Prices are listed in EUR and include VAT where applicable. Alison Resin Creations reserves the right to adjust pricing for future releases or custom work. Shipping is calculated at checkout.

Shipping & Delivery

Ready-made pieces ship within five working days. Custom designs ship once photos are approved and resin has fully cured. Tracking details are provided for all parcels. Alison Resin Creations is not responsible for delays once parcels enter the postal system.

Returns

Due to the bespoke nature of resin art, returns are accepted only in cases of transit damage or workmanship errors. Please contact the studio within 7 days of delivery with photos so we can arrange a repair or replacement.

Care

Resin is durable but not indestructible. Keep out of extreme heat, avoid prolonged direct sunlight, and wipe gently with a soft cloth.

Contact
