Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and protect your data when you browse, shop, or contact Alison Resin Creations.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide during checkout (name, delivery address, email) to fulfil orders. Optional custom requests may include reference photos, preferred colour palettes, and wording.

How We Use Your Data

Order information is used to process payment, pack, and ship your items. Contact details are used to send order updates. Custom order details are used solely for the requested piece.

Payments

Payments are processed by Shopify. Alison Resin Creations does not store your card details.

Communication

We may email you with order confirmations, dispatch updates, or follow-up questions for custom orders. You can request data deletion at any time.

Data Retention

Order records are kept for accounting and legal purposes. Custom reference images are deleted after your piece reaches you.

Contact

Email hello@alisonresincreations.com for privacy questions, data access, or removal requests.
