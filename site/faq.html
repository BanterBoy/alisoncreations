How We Sell

Key information on ordering, payments, custom requests, and caring for your resin art.

How do I buy?

Add any design to your cart and proceed to checkout. Shopify securely processes card payments. Local customers can add a note at checkout to arrange collection instead of shipping.

Are pieces ready to post?

Most pieces listed in the shop are in stock and ready to ship within five working days. Custom colourways or quote plaques may require an extra 7–10 days for curing and finishing.

Can I request a custom order?

Yes! Use the contact page with details about colours, wording, and timelines. Alison will confirm feasibility, pricing, and expected dispatch before invoicing.

How much is shipping?

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout and include protective packaging. Orders over €80 ship free within Ireland.

How should I care for resin art?

Display pieces away from direct heat or prolonged UV exposure. Wipe gently with a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners and do not fully submerge in water.

Questions?

Send a message via contact page or email hello@alisonresincreations.com.
