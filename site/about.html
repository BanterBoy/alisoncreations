About Alison Resin Creations

A small Irish studio turning glittering colour stories and miniature worlds into keepsake resin art.

Alison Resin Creations began as an experiment with resin, pigments, and light. Alison loved how resin could capture sparkles, botanicals, or tiny characters within a glossy, glass-like finish. Friends and family started requesting personalised cottages, celebration plaques, and memorial ornaments — and the hobby grew into a full studio.

Each piece is poured, demoulded, sanded, and finished by hand. Layered pours create depth, while carefully placed inclusions add meaning: crystals for good luck, glow-in-the-dark powders for night-time magic, or hand-painted florals for extra detail.

Today the collection spans light-up homes, quote plaques, fairy folk, and pet memorials. Many designs are limited runs, and Alison loves collaborating on bespoke requests. Every order is carefully packaged with eco-conscious materials and a handwritten thank-you note.

If you would like a colourway that is not currently listed, or you need a special message embedded within your piece, please reach out through the contact page. Alison will confirm availability and timing for your custom creation.
